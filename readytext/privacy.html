ReadyText Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the ReadyText app (package com.longthuy.readytext, distributed on Google Play) handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Who we are

ReadyText is published by Long Thuy Pty Ltd (ABN 15 161 126 647), 375 Pakington Street, Newtown VIC 3220, Australia ("we", "us").

The short version

  • ReadyText has no account and no server of ours. We do not collect your customers' names, phone numbers, messages, templates or history. They are stored only on your phone.
  • ReadyText does not send messages by itself. For each customer it opens your SMS app (or WhatsApp) with the message filled in, and you tap Send there.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ads software collects some device information (such as the advertising ID and IP address) to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it) you are asked for consent first.
  • Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Information stored only on your phone

ReadyText keeps the following in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and the app does not upload it anywhere:

  • Customer lists you enter: phone numbers and names that you paste, pick from your contacts, import from a CSV file or share into the app, plus an amount if your CSV file has one.
  • Remembered customer names: when a number has a name, the app remembers it so that the name appears the next time you paste that number.
  • Message templates that you write or copy from the built-in ones.
  • Send history: for each message, the customer's number and name, the template name, the message text, the channel (SMS or WhatsApp), the result (sent, skipped or failed) and the time.
  • A sending session in progress, so that sending can continue if Android closes the app.
  • Settings: shop name, review link, language, theme, default channel, the time the app was first opened, the last time a full-screen ad was shown, and whether Premium is active (so the app starts correctly offline).

Device backup. Your customers' numbers, names and send history are never included in Android's cloud backup to your Google account. If you have turned on Android backup, only the app's settings (such as your shop name, review link, language and theme) may be backed up. When you move to a new phone with a direct phone-to-phone transfer, your customer data and history can move with it. We have no access to any backup.

2. Sending messages

When you send, ReadyText opens your default SMS app or WhatsApp with the customer's number and your message already filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap Send in that app. The message is then delivered by that app and by your mobile carrier or WhatsApp under their own terms and privacy policies.

The Google Play version of ReadyText does not ask for SMS permissions. It cannot send SMS by itself and cannot read your messages.

3. Contacts and files

  • Contacts: the Contacts button opens Android's contact picker. ReadyText only receives the one contact you choose (name and phone number). It does not have permission to read your address book.
  • Import CSV: you choose a file with Android's file picker. ReadyText reads only that file (up to 2 MB) on your phone.
  • Export CSV (Premium): ReadyText writes your history only to the place you choose.
  • Share into ReadyText: text that you share from another app is placed in the customer list on your phone.
  • WhatsApp check: the app checks whether WhatsApp is installed, only to decide whether to show the WhatsApp option. This is not sent anywhere.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad on the Send and History screens and sometimes a full-screen ad after a batch of messages is finished (never while you are sending, and at most once every 10 minutes). Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects and processes information such as:

  • the advertising ID and app set ID of your device;
  • device information such as model, Android version, language and screen size;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city);
  • interactions with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and app launches;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the ads SDK, such as crash and error logs.

Google may share this information with advertisers and ad partners as described in its policies. Google uses it under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

ReadyText never gives AdMob your customers' names, numbers, messages, templates or history.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in other places where the law requires it, ReadyText shows Google's consent message before ads are requested. Whether ads are personalised depends on your choice. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy options (shown when it applies to you).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Premium: with Premium, ReadyText does not start the ads SDK and does not request or show ads. (If you buy Premium during a session in which ads were already running, the ads stop immediately.)

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

ReadyText Premium is sold through Google Play as a monthly subscription (with a free trial where offered) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play handles the payment under the Google Play terms and Google's privacy policy. We never receive your card or bank details.

The app asks Google Play on your phone which ReadyText purchases you own, so it can turn on Premium. It stores only whether Premium is active. Google gives us limited order information through Google Play Console (such as order number, product, price, date and country), which we use only for accounting, refunds and support.

To cancel a subscription, use Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or Settings > Manage subscription in ReadyText.

7. No analytics

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal information. The only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (with Google's consent tool) and Google Play Billing.

8. Children

ReadyText is a tool for businesses and is not directed to children. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has used the app in a way that gave us their information, contact us and we will help.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

Information on your phone stays there until you delete it:

  • History > Clear history deletes all send records. You can also delete templates one by one, and Clear empties the customer list on the Send screen.
  • Remembered customer names, settings and everything else are deleted when you clear the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > ReadyText > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall the app.

We hold no copy of this information, so we cannot restore it for you.

Information collected by Google for ads is kept by Google under its own retention rules. You can reset or delete your advertising ID as described above. Order records from Google Play are kept by us only as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (and the Australian Privacy Principles), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit some uses of it, and to withdraw consent.

Because ReadyText keeps your data on your phone, you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app. For ad data, use the options in section 4 or contact Google. For anything else, write to us at the address in section 14. We will reply within 30 days.

For the GDPR: ads are personalised only with your consent; non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests in funding a free app; purchases are handled to perform our contract with you.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (www.oaic.gov.au) or to the data protection authority where you live.

11. Security and international transfers

App data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Google's SDKs send data to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google may process data in countries other than yours, under its own safeguards.

12. Your customers

Your customers' details stay on your phone. Please only message customers who expect to hear from you, and follow the messaging and anti-spam rules in your country (for example, the Spam Act 2003 in Australia).

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
